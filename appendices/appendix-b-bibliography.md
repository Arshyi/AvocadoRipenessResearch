# Appendix B — Evidence-Audited Bibliography

**Verification date:** 30 July 2026

## Evidence labels

- **[V-P]** Bibliographic metadata verified against a publisher, DOI, institutional, or repository primary source.
- **[V-L]** Methods or results also checked in a locally supplied full-text PDF.
- **[R]** Repository or dataset record; access and licensing must be read independently of the associated article.
- **[U]** A reuse licence, raw-data release, or another stated field remains unresolved.

DOI links are used as persistent primary routes. A paper’s open-access licence does not automatically license a separately hosted raw dataset, and the existence of a public repository does not by itself grant reuse or redistribution rights.
